A total of 7 Red-Eyes support cards made by Beil196/livmorgan and scripted/revised by me (XAX4). The effects are 95% as they worded them and are not made for balance. Enjoy.

Included cards:
  Red-Eyes Shadow Lord (livmorgan)
  
    DARK Dragon
    Xyz Effect
    Rank 7
    2 level 7 monsters	
    You can only Special Summon "Red-Eyes Shadow Lord" once per turn. 
    If this card is Xyz Summoned: You can Special Summon 1 "Red-Eyes" monster from your Deck, GY, or banishment. 
    You can detach 2 materials from this card; Special Summon 1 "Red-Eyes" Xyz Monster from your Extra Deck by using this face-up card as material (this is treated as an Xyz Summon, transfer this card's materials to the Summoned monster).
    
  Crimson Eyes Abyssal Dragon (Beil196)
  
    DARK Dragon
    Synchro Effect
    Level 9
    1 Tuner + 1+ non-Tuner "Red-Eyes" monsters 
    (This card is always treated as a "Red-Eyes" card) 
    Your opponent cannot activate effects in the GY. 
    Once per turn (Quick-Effect): You can pay 2000 LP; banish all cards in your opponent's GY.

  Red-Eyes Vanguard (livmorgan)

    DARK Dragon
    Effect
    Level 6
    You can only use each of the following effects of "Red-Eyes Vanguard" once per turn. 
    If you control a "Red-Eyes" monster or a level 7 Normal Monster: You can Special Summon this card (from your hand). 
    During your Main Phase: You can send 1 "Red-Eyes" card or a card that mentions "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" from your Deck to the GY, and if you do, increase this card's level by 1. 
    You cannot Special Summon from the Extra Deck for the rest of this turn after this effect resolves, except for Fusion and Xyz Monsters.

  Blazing Eyes Black Dragon (Beil196)

    DARK Dragon
    Fusion Effect
    Level 12
    2 "Red-Eyes" Fusion Monsters	
    (This card is always treated as a "Red-Eyes" card and its name becomes "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" while in play)	
    Unaffected by other cards' effects. 
    If a monster your opponent controls activates an effect: Change that monster's ATK/DEF to 0 until the end of this turn. 
    You can only use each of the following effects of "Blazing Eyes Black Dragon" once per turn. 
    You can Special Summon 1 "Red-Eyes" monster from your hand or GY, and if you do, you can equip 1 "Red-Eyes" card or 1 DARK Dragon monster from your hand, Deck or GY to it as an Equip Spell. 
    You can Tribute 1 other monster you control; Special Summon 1 monster from your Deck that lists "Max Metalmorph" in its card text for its Special Summon condition, ignoring its Summoning conditions. 
    (Quick-Effect) You can target 1 Level 7 or higher "Red-Eyes" monster you control; Xyz Summon 1 "Red-Eyes" Xyz Monster from your Extra Deck, using that target as material (this is treated as an Xyz Summon).

  Red-Eyes Cosmic Dragon (Beil196)

    DARK Dragon
    Link Effect
    Link 3
    2+ monsters including a "Red-Eyes" monster	
    You can only activate each of the following effects of "Red-Eyes Cosmic Dragon" once per turn. 
    (Quick Effect) You can negate the effects of all face-up monsters your opponent currently controls until the end of the next turn. 
    (Quick Effect) You can target 1 card on the field; until the end of this turn, that target becomes unaffected by other cards' effects. 
    If this Link Summoned card leaves the field: You can Special Summon 1 "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" from your hand, Deck, or GY.

  Abyss Eyes Primal Dragon (Beil196)

    DARK Dragon
    Link Effect
    Link 4
    2+ Effect Monsters including a "Red-Eyes" monster	
    (This card is always treated as a "Red-Eyes" card and its name becomes "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" while in play)	
    When this card is Link Summoned: Pay half your LP; destroy all cards your opponent controls. 
    Once per turn (Quick Effect): You can Tribute 1 monster you control; this card gains ATK equal to the Tributed monster's original ATK.

  Meteor Black Skull Dragon (Beil196)

    DARK Dragon
    Effect
    Level 10
    You can Special Summon this card from your Deck by Tributing 1 "Red-Eyes Archfiend of Lighting" and 1 "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" you control. 
    When this card is Summoned: Destroy all Spell & Traps your opponent controls. 
    You can banish this card in the GY; Special Summon 1 "Red-Eyes Archfiend of Lightning" and 1 "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" from your Deck or GY.
    You can only activate this effect of "Meteor Black Skull Dragon" once per turn.

  Tyranny with Eyes of Red (Beil196)

    Quick-Play Spell
    Banish 2 other cards from your hand, field, and/or GY, face-down; Special Summon up to 3 "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" from your Deck and/or GY. 
    Once this effect resolves, banish this card, face-down. 
    You cannot Special Summon monsters from the Extra Deck during the turn you activate this card.

  Red-Eyes Sacrifice (Beil196)

    Quick-Play Spell
    Pay half your Life points and Tribute 1 level 7 monster you control; Special Summon 1 monster from your Extra Deck, ignoring its Summoning conditions. 
    You cannot Normal or Special Summon monsters for the rest of this turn after this effect resolves.

  Whelp with Eyes of Red (Beil196)

    DARK Dragon
    Link Effect
    Link 2
    2 monsters including at least 1 "Red-Eyes" monster	
    (This card is always treated as a "Red-Eyes" card)	
    Once per turn: You can declare a level between 1 and 12; change the level of all monsters you control to be that level until the End Phase. 
    If this card leaves the field: Special Summon 1 Level 3 or lower "Red-Eyes" monster from your hand, Deck or GY.

  Demand of the Red-Eyes (livmorgan)

    Normal Spell
    Discard 1 card; add 1 "Red-Eyes" monster and a Spell or Trap that mentions "Red-Eyes" that isn't "Demand of the Red-Eyes" from your Deck to your hand. 
    You can only activate 1 "Demand of the Red-Eyes" per turn.

  Red-Eyes Tyrant Dragon (Beil196)
  
    DARK Dragon
    Fusion Effect
    Level 10
    1 "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" equipped with a "Red-Eyes" monster	
    This card can be Special Summoned from your Extra Deck by Tributing the above monster you control (you do not use "Polymerization"). 
    Your opponent must attack this card if able. 
    When this card is targeted for an attack: This card gains ATK equal to the original ATK of the attacking monster until the end of the Battle Phase.

  Red-Eyes Dark Flare Dragon (Beil196)
  
    DARK Dragon
    Xyz Effect
    Rank 10
    3 level 7 "Red-Eyes" monsters	
    You can also Xyz Summon this card by using a "Red-Eyes Flare Metal Dragon" you control as material (transfer its materials to this card). 
    Once per turn (Quick-Effect): You can detach 1 material from this card and target 1 card your opponent controls; destroy it. 
    If this card battles a Defense Position monster, inflict piercing battle damage. 
    If this card in its owner's possession is destroyed by battle or an opponent's card effect while it has material: You can Special Summon 1 "Red-Eyes" monster from your GY. 
    This card gains 300 ATK for each "Red-Eyes" monster on the field and in either GY.

  Spirit with Eyes of Red (Beil196)
  
    DARK Dragon
    Tuner Effect
    Level 2
    (This card is always treated as a "Red-Eyes" card) 
    If you control no monsters, you can Special Summon this card (from your hand). 
    You can pay 1000 LP, then Tribute this card; Special Summon 2 level 3 or lower monsters that mention "Red-Eyes" from your Deck or GY.

  Stone of the Red-Eyes (livmorgan)

    DARK Dragon
    Effect
    Level 1
    You can only use each of the following effects of "Stone of the Red-Eyes" once per turn. 
    If this card is sent from the field to the GY as material for a "Red-Eyes" Link Monster: You can add 1 "Red-Eyes" Spell/Trap Card from your Deck to your hand. 
    If "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" is Special Summoned while this card is in your GY: You can Special Summon this card, and if you do, its level becomes 7, but when this card would leave the field, banish it instead.

  Domain of the Black Dragon (livmorgan)

    Field Spell
    You can only activate "Domain of the Black Dragon" once per turn.

    Once per turn you can send 1 "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" or a card that mentions it from your Deck to the GY.

    DARK Dragon

  Red-Eyes Necromancer (Beil196)
  
    DARK Spellcaster
    Effect
    Level 7

    You can only use each of the following effects of "Red-Eyes Necromancer" once per turn.

    Xyz Effect
    Rank 7

  Red-Eyes Full Metal Flare Dragon (Beil196)
  
    DARK Dragon
    Xyz Effect
    Rank 8
    3 level 8 DARK monsters	
    You can also Xyz Summon this card by using a "Red-Eyes Flare Metal Dragon" you control as material (transfer its materials to this card). 
    This card cannot be targeted by or destroyed by card effects while you control another "Red-Eyes" monster that was Special Summoned from the Extra Deck. 
    Once per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card; Special Summon an Xyz Monster from your Extra Deck, then attach the detached material to that card as material (this is treated as an Xyz Summon).
