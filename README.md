A total of 7 Red-Eyes support cards made by Beil196 and scripted by me (XAX4). The effects are 95% as he worded them and are not made for balance. Enjoy.

Included cards:
  Crimson Eyes Abyssal Dragon
    DARK Dragon
    Synchro Effect
    Level 9
    1 Tuner + 1+ non-Tuner "Red-Eyes" monsters	
    (This card is always treated as a "Red-Eyes" card) Your opponent cannot activate effects in the GY. Once per turn (Quick-Effect): 
    You can pay 2000 LP; banish all cards in your opponent's GY.

  Red-Eyes Tyrant Dragon
    DARK Dragon
    Fusion Effect
    Level 10
    1 "Red-Eyes Black Dragon" equipped with a "Red-Eyes" monster	
    This card can be Special Summoned from your Extra Deck by Tributing the above monster you control (you do not use "Polymerization"). 
    Your opponent must attack this card if able. When this card is targeted for an attack (Quick-Effect): This card gains ATK equal to the 
    original ATK of the attacking monster until the end of the Battle Phase.

  Red-Eyes Dark Flare Dragon
    DARK Dragon
    Xyz Effect
    Rank 10
    3 level 7 "Red-Eyes" monsters	
    You can also Xyz Summon this card by using a "Red-Eyes Flare Metal Dragon" you control as material (transfer its materials to this card). 
    Once per turn (Quick-Effect): You can detach 1 material from this card and target 1 card your opponent controls; destroy it. If this card 
    battles a Defense Position monster, inflict piercing battle damage. If this card in its owner's possession is destroyed by battle or an 
    opponent's card effect while it has material: You can Special Summon 1 "Red-Eyes" monster from your GY. This card gains 300 ATK for each 
    "Red-Eyes" monster on the field and in either GY.

  Spirit with Eyes of Red
    DARK Dragon
    Tuner Effect
    Level 2
    (This card is always treated as a "Red-Eyes" card) If you control no monsters, you can Special Summon this card (from your hand). You can 
    pay 1000 LP, then Tribute this card; Special Summon 2 level 3 or lower "Red-Eyes" monsters or monsters that mention "Red-Eyes" from your Deck 
    or GY.

  Red-Eyes Necroflare Dragon
    DARK Zombie
    Fusion Effect
    Level 7
    1 "Red-Eyes" monster + 2 DARK Dragon monsters		
    If this card is Special Summoned: You can target 1 monster on the field or in either GY; equip it to this card as an Equip Spell, and if you do, 
    Special Summon 1 monster from either GY. This effect cannot be negated. Once per turn (Quick-Effect): You can equip 1 "Red-Eyes" or Equip Spell 
    Card from your GY or banishment to this card. If this card leaves the field by battle or an opponent's card effect, you can activate this effect: 
    Return this card to the Extra Deck; equip a face-up card your opponent controls to a monster you control.

  Red-Eyes Necromancer
    DARK Spellcaster
    Effect
    Level 7
    You can Special Summon this card from your hand or GY by banishing 1 card from your hand, field, or GY. For the rest of this turn after Summoning 
    this card this way, you cannot Special Summon monsters, except DARK Spellcaster, Dragon, and Zombie monsters. You can only Special Summon "Red-Eyes 
    Necromancer" once per turn this way. You can only use each of the following effects of "Red-Eyes Necromancer" once per turn. If this card is Special 
    Summoned: You can add 1 "Red-Eyes" card from your Deck or GY to your hand. During your opponent's turn (Quick-Effect): You can banish 1 "Fusion" or 
    "Polymerization" Spell Card from your field or GY; apply that card's effect as if you activated it.

  Red-Eyes Full Metal Flare Dragon
    DARK Dragon
    Xyz Effect
    Rank 8
    3 level 8 DARK monsters	
    You can also Xyz Summon this card by using a "Red-Eyes Flare Metal Dragon" you control as material (transfer its materials to this card). This card 
    cannot be targeted by or destroyed by card effects while you control another "Red-Eyes" monster that was Special Summoned from the Extra Deck. Once 
    per turn: You can detach 1 material from this card; Special Summon an Xyz Monster from your Extra Deck, then attach the detached material to that 
    card as material (this is treated as an Xyz Summon). Once per turn when your opponent activates a card or effect (Quick-Effect): You can detach 1 
    material from this card; negate the activation and destroy that card, and if you do, inflict 500 damage to your opponent for each "Red-Eyes" monster 
    you control.
